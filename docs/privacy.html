Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

MultiEngines Search is a Chrome extension published by an independent developer. This page describes how the extension handles information.

What the extension does

The extension adds a search-engine picker on Google and can open a new tab on google.com. When you search, the browser goes directly to Google, Bing, Yahoo, or DuckDuckGo. The extension does not proxy your searches through a developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

The only preference saved is the default search engine you choose in Options (and the engine selected for the current tab while you use it). That value is stored locally with Chrome’s chrome.storage API. It does not leave your browser as part of this extension.

What we do not collect

  • Search queries are not sent to the developer.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, or usage telemetry.
  • There is no account, and no data is sold or shared with third parties by us.

The search engines you visit (Google, Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo) have their own policies. This document covers only MultiEngines Search.

New Tab

If you keep the New Tab override, a new tab loads google.com in your browser. That request is between you and Google, not a service we run.

Permissions

The extension uses storage to remember your engine choice and declarativeNetRequest so a Google search URL can open on the engine you selected. It does not use those permissions to send data to the developer.

How to delete local data

Remove the extension in Chrome. Chrome then deletes the extension’s local storage.
